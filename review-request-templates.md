# Review Request Templates — Trustman Roofing LLC

Use these templates to ask satisfied customers for Google reviews. Reviews are the #1 factor in local search ranking — even 5–10 solid reviews will put you ahead of most local competitors.

---

## How to Get Your Google Review Link

Once your Google Business Profile is verified:

### Method 1: From Google Business Dashboard (Easiest)
1. Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com)
2. Select your Trustman Roofing LLC profile
3. In the left menu, look for **"Ask for reviews"** (or **"Get more reviews"**)
4. Google will generate a **short link** — copy it
5. It will look something like: `https://g.page/r/XXXXXXXXX/review`

### Method 2: From Google Search
1. Search Google for **"Trustman Roofing LLC"** while signed into your business Google account
2. Your Business Profile manager panel should appear on the right
3. Click **"Ask for reviews"** → copy the link

### Method 3: Create a Short Link
1. Once you have the review link, you can use [bitly.com](https://bitly.com) to shorten it
2. Example custom short link: `bit.ly/trustman-review`
3. This makes it easier to text and remember

### Save Your Link
Once you have it, paste it here for reference:

> **My Google Review Link:** `[PASTE YOUR LINK HERE]`

## Template 1: Casual Text Message (Best for recent customers)

> Hey [FIRST NAME]! It's Jason from Trustman Roofing. Hope the new roof is treating you well! 🏠
>
> If you've got a minute, it'd mean a lot if you could leave us a quick Google review. It really helps other homeowners find us.
>
> Here's the link: [GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]
>
> Thanks so much — really appreciated working with you!

### Tips:
- Send within **1–2 weeks** of project completion (while the experience is fresh)
- Personalize it — mention the specific work if possible ("Hope the new shingle roof looks great!")
- Text works better than email for response rate
- Best times to send: **Tuesday–Thursday, 10am–2pm or 6–8pm**

### Tips for Follow-Ups:
- **Only follow up once.** Two messages max (original + one nudge). Never nag.
- Wait **5–7 days** before sending the nudge.
- If they don't respond to the nudge, let it go gracefully.

## Bonus: After Completing a Job (In-Person Ask)

The absolute best time to ask is **right after the job is done** and the customer is happy. Here's a casual script:

> "Hey [NAME], really glad you're happy with how everything turned out. If you get a chance, a Google review would mean the world to us — it's honestly the best way to help a small business. I can text you the link right now if that's cool?"

Then text them **Template 1** on the spot.

## Review Response Templates

**Always respond to every review** — Google rewards engagement and it shows prospective customers you care.

### Responding to a 5-Star Review:

> Thank you so much, [NAME]! It was great working with you, and we're glad you're happy with the results. If you ever need anything down the road, don't hesitate to give us a call. We appreciate the trust! — Jason

### Responding to a 4-Star Review:

> Thanks for the kind words, [NAME]! We're glad we could help. If there's anything we could have done better, we'd love to hear about it — always looking to improve. Thanks for choosing Trustman Roofing!

### Responding to a Negative Review:

> Hi [NAME], I'm sorry to hear about your experience. That's not the standard we hold ourselves to. I'd really like to make this right — could you give me a call at 636-541-7005 or email jason@trustmanroofing.com? I want to understand what happened and see how we can fix it. — Jason Talarski, Owner

> **Important:** Never argue in public reviews. Be professional, empathetic, and take it offline. Other customers are reading your response more than the reviewer's complaint.
