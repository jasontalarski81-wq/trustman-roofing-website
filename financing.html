Step 4: Get Approved & Work Begins

Once approved, we schedule your roof installation at a time that works for you. Most residential roof replacements are completed in just 1–3 days. You don't pay anything upfront — your financing covers the project cost, and your first payment isn't due until after the work is done.

Step 5: Pay Over Time — Interest-Free

Spread your payments across 21 months with zero interest. As long as the balance is paid within the promotional period, you pay exactly what your roof costs — nothing more. It's that simple.

Why Financing Your Roof Makes Sense

Some homeowners hesitate to finance a roof, thinking they should save up and pay cash. While that's certainly an option, here's why financing often makes more sense:

Don't Wait on a Damaged Roof

A damaged roof gets worse every day. What starts as minor hail damage or a small leak can escalate into rotted decking, mold growth, and interior water damage — all of which are far more expensive to fix than the roof itself. Financing lets you address the problem now, before it compounds into a much larger expense.

Preserve Your Savings

With 21 months of interest-free payments, you can keep your emergency fund and savings intact while still getting a quality roof installed. There's no financial penalty for spreading the cost over time — zero interest means zero extra cost.

Take Advantage of Insurance + Financing

If your roof damage is covered by your homeowner's insurance, your insurance payment covers a significant portion of the cost. Financing is perfect for covering the remaining out-of-pocket expenses — your deductible, any upgrades you choose (like impact-resistant shingles or enhanced ventilation), and items that may not be included in your insurance settlement. The combination of insurance coverage and interest-free financing means you can get a premium roof restoration with minimal financial strain.

Increase Your Home's Value

A new roof is one of the highest-ROI home improvements you can make. According to industry data, a new roof can recoup 60–70% or more of its cost in increased home value. Financing lets you make that investment now — boosting your home's curb appeal, energy efficiency, and resale value — without depleting your cash reserves.

No-Money-Down Options

Trustman Roofing offers no-money-down financing for qualified homeowners. That means you can get your roof replaced without any upfront payment. Your first financing payment begins after the work is completed, giving you time to plan your budget around manageable monthly installments.

For homeowners with insurance claims, this is especially powerful: your insurance payments arrive over time (initial ACV check, then recoverable depreciation after completion), and your financing payments align with that timeline. You're never stuck paying large sums out of pocket while waiting for insurance reimbursement.

How Financing Works Alongside Insurance Claims

Many of our customers use financing in combination with their insurance claim. Here's a common scenario:

  1. Your roof is damaged by a hailstorm and your insurance approves a full replacement.
  2. Your insurance company issues the initial ACV payment (replacement cost minus depreciation, minus your deductible).
  3. You use financing to cover your deductible and any upgrades — no out-of-pocket expense at the time of installation.
  4. Trustman Roofing completes your roof replacement.
  5. You submit the final invoice to your insurance company and receive the recoverable depreciation payment.
  6. You apply the depreciation payment toward your financing balance, reducing or eliminating your remaining payments.

This approach means you never have to write a large check or drain your savings account. The financing bridges the gap between what insurance pays upfront and the full cost of your new roof.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there really no interest?

Yes. Our 21-month promotional financing carries zero interest as long as the balance is paid within the 21-month promotional period. You pay only the cost of your roof — nothing more.

What if I can pay it off early?

There are no prepayment penalties. You can pay off your balance at any time — whether that's month one or month twenty-one. Many homeowners pay off their balance early when their insurance depreciation check arrives.

Does applying affect my credit score?

The initial consultation and discussion about financing options does not affect your credit. A credit inquiry occurs when you formally submit a financing application, which is standard for any financing product.

Can I finance just my deductible?

Yes. You can finance any portion of your roofing project — whether that's the full cost, just your insurance deductible, or specific upgrades you'd like to add beyond what insurance covers.

How do I apply?

The easiest way to get started is to schedule your free inspection. During your appointment, we'll review your roof's condition, provide an estimate, and walk you through the financing application on the spot. Or call us at 636-541-7005 to discuss your options over the phone.

What if I'm not approved?

We work with multiple financing partners to offer a range of options. If one program isn't the right fit, we'll explore alternatives with you. Our goal is to help you find a way to protect your home without financial hardship.
