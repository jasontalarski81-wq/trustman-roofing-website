## 5. Step-by-Step: Claim or Create Your Google Business Profile

### If the business already exists on Google Maps:

1. **Go to** [google.com/business](https://google.com/business) and sign in with your Google account (or create one using jason@trustmanroofing.com).
2. **Search** for "Trustman Roofing LLC" in the search bar.
3. If it appears, click on it and select **"Claim this business"** or **"Own this business?"**.
4. Google will ask you to **verify ownership** — typically by:
   - **Phone call or text** to 636-541-7005
   - **Postcard** mailed to your business address (takes 5–14 days)
   - **Email** verification (sometimes available)
   - **Video verification** (Google may ask you to record a short video of your business location/signage)
5. Follow the prompts to complete verification.

### If the business does NOT exist yet:

1. **Go to** [google.com/business](https://google.com/business) → click **"Manage now"**.
2. **Enter your business name:** `Trustman Roofing LLC`
3. **Select your category:** `Roofing Contractor`
4. **Add a location?**
   - If you have a physical office/shop customers visit → **Yes**, enter the address.
   - If you go to customers (service-area business) → Select **"I deliver goods and services to my customers"** and choose **no** for a storefront location.
5. **Set your service area:**
   - Add: `St. Louis, MO`, `Arnold, MO`, `O'Fallon, MO`
   - You can add more cities/zip codes later.
6. **Add contact info:**
   - Phone: `636-541-7005`
   - Website: `https://trustmanroofing.com`
7. **Verify your business** (see methods above).
8. **Once verified, complete your profile:**
   - Add the **full business description** from Section 1 above.
   - Upload **photos** (at least 5–10 to start):
     - Logo
     - Team photo / Jason headshot
     - Before & after project photos
     - Truck/vehicle with branding

   - Set **business hours** (or mark as "By appointment").
   - Add all **services** from Section 3.
   - Post the **FAQ** from Section 4.

## 6. Weekly Google Posts — Tips & Ideas

Google lets you publish posts directly on your Business Profile. These show up when people find you on Google Search and Maps. **Posting weekly is one of the easiest ways to boost your local ranking.**

### How to Post:
1. Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com)
2. Select your business → click **"Add update"** (or "Posts" in the left menu)
3. Choose a post type: **Update**, **Offer**, or **Event**
4. Add a photo, text, and a call-to-action button (e.g., "Call now", "Learn more")
5. Publish!

### Weekly Post Ideas (Rotating Calendar):

| Week | Post Idea | CTA Button |
|------|-----------|------------|
| 1 | Before & after project photo with a short story | "Call now" |
| 2 | Storm season tip (e.g., "5 signs your roof has hail damage") | "Learn more" → blog post |
| 3 | Customer testimonial or review highlight | "Call now" |
| 4 | Financing promotion ("21 months interest-free — protect your home today") | "Call now" |
| 5 | Behind-the-scenes / team spotlight | "Learn more" |
| 6 | FAQ answer (pull from Section 4 above) | "Call now" |
| 7 | Seasonal tip (winter prep, spring inspection, etc.) | "Book online" |
| 8 | Community involvement / local event | "Learn more" |

### Post Best Practices:
- **Include a photo every time** — posts with images get significantly more engagement.
- **Keep text to 150–300 words** — enough to be helpful, short enough to read.
- **Always include a CTA button** — "Call now" is your best friend.
- **Use local keywords naturally:** "roof replacement in Arnold MO", "St. Louis storm damage repair", etc.
- **Post at least once a week** — consistency matters more than perfection.
- **Respond to every review** (positive and negative) — this counts as engagement too.
