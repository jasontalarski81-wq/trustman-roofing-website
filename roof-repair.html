Common Roof Repairs We Handle

St. Louis roofs face a lot of punishment year-round. Here are the most common repair issues we see and fix:

Leak Repair & Detection

Roof leaks are the number one reason homeowners call us. A leak can originate far from where the water stain appears on your ceiling — water travels along rafters, sheathing, and insulation before it finds a place to drip. Our inspectors trace the leak back to its source using a combination of interior attic inspection, exterior roof evaluation, and — when needed — controlled water testing.

Common causes of roof leaks include damaged flashing, cracked pipe boots, deteriorated sealant around vents, ice dam formation, and missing or broken shingles. We identify the root cause and fix it properly so the leak doesn't come back.

Storm Damage Repair

After a severe storm rolls through the St. Louis metro, your roof may sustain damage from hail, high winds, or fallen tree branches. Storm damage can range from a few displaced shingles to large sections of exposed decking. Trustman Roofing responds quickly to storm damage calls, performing emergency tarping when needed and scheduling permanent repairs as soon as conditions allow.

If your storm damage is extensive enough to warrant a full replacement, we'll let you know honestly and help you explore your options, including insurance claim assistance.

Shingle Repair & Replacement

Individual shingles can crack, curl, blow off, or lose their protective granule coating over time. Replacing damaged shingles promptly prevents water from penetrating the underlayment and decking below. We carry a wide selection of shingle colors and styles to match your existing roof as closely as possible.

Flashing Repair

Flashing is the thin metal material installed at roof transitions — around chimneys, skylights, dormers, walls, and in valleys. When flashing corrodes, lifts, or separates, it creates direct pathways for water to enter your home. Flashing repair is one of the most critical yet frequently overlooked roof repairs. Our crews remove the compromised flashing, prepare the surface properly, and install new flashing with appropriate sealant for a watertight finish.

Pipe Boot & Vent Repair

Every plumbing vent, exhaust fan, and HVAC penetration on your roof has a boot or collar that seals around it. These rubber and neoprene components degrade from UV exposure and temperature cycling, eventually cracking and allowing water to drip directly into your attic. We replace pipe boots and vent seals as part of our standard repair service.

Gutter & Fascia Repair

Damaged gutters and rotted fascia boards compromise your roof's drainage system, leading to water backup under the roof edge and potential soffit or wall damage. We repair or replace gutters, fascia, and drip edge as needed to ensure your roofing system functions as a complete unit.

Repair vs. Replace: How Do You Know?

One of the most important questions homeowners face is whether to repair or replace their roof. Here are guidelines to help you decide:

Repair makes sense when:

  • The damage is localized to a small area (less than 30% of the roof surface).
  • Your roof is relatively young — under 15 years old for asphalt shingles.
  • The underlying decking and structure are sound.
  • You have no history of recurring leaks in different locations.
  • The repair cost is significantly less than a replacement.

Replacement makes sense when:

  • Damage is widespread across multiple areas of the roof.
  • Your roof is 20+ years old and showing systemic wear.
  • You're experiencing recurring leaks despite previous repairs.
  • The decking has soft spots, rot, or structural sagging.
  • An insurance claim covers a full roof replacement.

Trustman Roofing will always give you an honest recommendation. If a repair will solve your problem and extend your roof's life, that's what we'll suggest. We don't push replacements when they aren't needed.

  • Major repairs (large-area shingle replacement, decking repair, emergency tarping + permanent fix): Can range into the thousands, but still significantly less than a full replacement.
