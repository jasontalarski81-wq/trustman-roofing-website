When Is It Time to Replace Your Roof?

Your roof is the first line of defense against St. Louis's harsh weather — from scorching summer heat to ice storms in winter. But even the best roof won't last forever. Knowing when it's time for a full roof replacement can save you thousands in interior water damage, mold remediation, and emergency repairs.

Here are the most common signs that your roof needs to be replaced:

  • Age: Most asphalt shingle roofs last 20–25 years. If your roof is approaching or past that age, it's time for a professional inspection.
  • Widespread shingle damage: Curling, cracking, buckling, or missing shingles across large areas indicate the roofing material has reached the end of its lifespan.
  • Recurring leaks: A single leak can often be patched, but multiple leaks or leaks that keep coming back usually mean the underlayment or decking is compromised.
  • Granule loss: Check your gutters. If they're filled with shingle granules, the protective layer is wearing away, leaving your roof vulnerable to UV damage and moisture.
  • Storm damage: Severe hail damage or wind damage can compromise your roof to the point where repairs aren't cost-effective.
  • Sagging roof deck: Visible sagging is a structural concern that requires immediate attention and almost always calls for a full replacement.
  • Daylight through the attic: If you can see light coming through the roof boards in your attic, moisture is getting in too.

If you've noticed any of these warning signs, schedule a free inspection with Trustman Roofing. We'll give you an honest assessment — no pressure, no obligation.

How Much Does a Roof Replacement Cost in St. Louis?

The cost of a roof replacement in the Greater St. Louis area depends on several factors:

  • Roof size: Measured in "squares" (100 sq. ft. each), larger roofs naturally cost more.
  • Material choice: Architectural shingles are the most affordable premium option, while metal and tile come at a higher price point with longer lifespans.
  • Roof complexity: Multiple slopes, valleys, dormers, skylights, and chimney flashing add labor and material costs.
  • Decking condition: If the plywood decking beneath your shingles is rotted or damaged, it needs to be replaced before new roofing goes on.
  • Ventilation: Proper attic ventilation is critical for roof longevity. Upgrading your ventilation system is sometimes necessary and adds to the project scope.
  • Permit and code requirements: Local building codes in St. Louis County and surrounding municipalities may require specific materials or installation methods.

We provide free, no-obligation estimates so you know exactly what your roof replacement will cost before any work begins.
